Prior publication approval does not transfer to the new project.

Check the portfolio catalog and relevant recorded decisions for duplication and reusable findings. Use any client posting supplied with this request as a demand signal; otherwise label evidence gaps honestly. Compare no more than three commercially relevant concepts that demonstrate a genuinely different capability. Use minimal delegation, one consolidated review and at most one repair pass. Present one recommendation for approval before building.

Read `docs/shipping-pipeline/REVIEWER_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md` during planning, carry it into the approved brief and active state's requiredFiles, and enforce it during verification and release. Each final project needs its own runnable demonstration, plain-English visual PDF and problem-solving evidence. Reuse the prior project's presentation techniques and components where useful, not its results or claims.

After concept approval, produce a bounded working proof, then seek expansion approval. Following expansion approval, build, test, assemble the evidence and Manifest, and request publication approval. Record actual outcomes and lessons for the next iteration. Keep updates concise and stop at approval boundaries, not in automatic candidate-rejection loops.

This file is an instruction entry point, not an unattended runner or authorization to publish, incur charges, or start the next iteration merely by reading it.
